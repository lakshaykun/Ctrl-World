Direction 2: Uncertainty-aware long-horizon prediction

Research question: Can the model estimate when its future predictions become unreliable?

Imagine:
- At 1 second, the prediction is reliable.
- At 5 seconds, some uncertainty appears.
- At 10 seconds, the predicted object position is incorrect.
Possible contribution:

Develop a reliability estimator that monitors prediction quality and determines when the model should use a shorter prediction horizon or re-anchor its context.

This could help prevent errors from accumulating.

Important: Uncertainty estimation and rollout correction already have related research, including recent work on re-denoising consistency. We would need a more specific contribution than simply adding uncertainty estimation.
