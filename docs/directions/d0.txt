Direction 1: Physical-consistency verification
My first choice

Research question: Can we detect physically incorrect generated robot interactions?

Imagine the robot moves towards a cup. The generated video shows the cup moving before the gripper touches it.

The video might look visually realistic, but the physical interaction is incorrect.

Possible contribution:
- Detect robot-object contact events.
- Track object movement across generated frames.
- Check whether object movement is consistent with the action and contact.
- Assign a physical-consistency score to each generated trajectory.
Why investigate this?

Ctrl-World already has action conditioning and multi-view generation. 
Instead of replacing its architecture, we could investigate an additional 
mechanism that verifies whether its predictions are physically credible.
